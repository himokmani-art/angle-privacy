Privacy Policy

ANGLE (“ANGLE” or the “App”) respects your privacy and uses only the information and permissions necessary to provide its features.

1. Personal Information We Collect

ANGLE does not require account registration and does not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number on external servers operated by ANGLE.

The App does not use tracking for advertising or user behavior analytics.

2. Camera and Microphone Use

ANGLE accesses your device camera to provide photo and video capture features.

When recording videos, ANGLE may access the microphone to record audio.

Photos and videos captured using ANGLE are processed on your device. ANGLE does not store or view them on external servers operated by ANGLE.

3. Photo Library Use

ANGLE may access the photo library so you can save captured photos and videos and review capture results in the App.

Photo library data is used only to provide these features.

4. Local Network and Device Connections

ANGLE uses local device-to-device connectivity to find nearby ANGLE devices and connect two iPhones for live camera viewing and remote capture features.

The following information may be exchanged between connected devices to provide these features:

  • The device display name set in ANGLE
  • Device connection status and camera/remote role information
  • Live camera video data
  • Remote capture commands and camera control information
  • Previews of photos and videos captured through ANGLE during the current connection, or capture results requested by the user

This information is used only to provide ANGLE device connection, live viewing, and remote capture features.

5. Capture Results and Connection Sessions

While using ANGLE remote capture, preview data for captured photos and videos may be shown on the connected device.

The capture review feature available during a connection is for reviewing results captured through ANGLE during the current connection session. It is not a feature for browsing the other device’s existing photo library.

Temporary previews and cache data created during a connection may be cleared when the connection ends, depending on app behavior.

Photos and videos saved to the camera device’s photo library may remain there until the user deletes them.

6. Stored Device Information

ANGLE may store the following information on your device to make future device connections easier:

  • An internal app identifier used to identify ANGLE devices
  • The ANGLE name set by the user
  • Display names for registered devices or aliases set by the user
  • Last connection date

This information is used to provide device connection and registered device management features and is not stored on external servers operated by ANGLE.

7. Third-Party Sharing and External Transfer

ANGLE does not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

The current version does not provide personal information to third parties for advertising or user tracking.

If advertising, analytics, or third-party services that process personal information are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and users will be notified as appropriate.

8. Retention and Deletion

App settings, registered device information, and other local data stored by ANGLE may remain on the device to provide app features.

You can remove local app data by using deletion features provided in the App or by deleting the App.

Photos and videos saved to the photo library are separate from ANGLE app data and can be managed directly in the Photos app.

9. Permission Management

You can change or revoke permissions granted to ANGLE, such as camera, microphone, photos, and local network access, in iOS Settings.

If you do not allow or later revoke certain permissions, features that require those permissions may not be available.

10. Children’s Privacy

ANGLE does not require account registration and does not provide features designed to collect or store children’s personal information on external servers operated by ANGLE.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may change if app features or privacy practices change.

If there are important changes, users may be notified through an appropriate method.
