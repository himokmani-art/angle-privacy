ANGLE Support

ANGLE lets you connect two nearby iPhones so you can view one iPhone's camera live and control photo and video capture from the other.

Getting Started

To use ANGLE's device connection and remote camera features, install and open ANGLE on two iPhones.

  1. Open ANGLE on both iPhones.
  2. Tap Connect on one iPhone.
  3. Select the nearby iPhone you want to connect to.
  4. Accept the connection request on the other iPhone.
  5. Once connected, you can use one iPhone as the camera device and the other for remote viewing and control.

Remote Camera

After connecting two iPhones, you can view the camera feed from the connected device and remotely control supported camera features.

Depending on the selected mode, you can take photos, record videos, adjust supported camera settings, and review capture results.

Switching Device Roles

While the devices are connected, you can switch which iPhone is used as the camera device and which iPhone is used for remote viewing and control.

Use the role-switching control in the connection management screen to change device roles.

Photos and Videos

Photos and videos captured with ANGLE can be saved to the photo library on the camera device.

Capture results can also be reviewed within ANGLE using the in-app gallery.

Permissions

ANGLE may request access to features such as the camera, microphone, photo library, and local network. These permissions are used only when required to provide the corresponding app features.

You can review or change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

If You Have Trouble Connecting

If another iPhone does not appear or a connection cannot be established, try the following:

  • Make sure ANGLE is open on both iPhones.
  • Make sure the required permissions for ANGLE have been allowed.
  • Close the connection screen and try searching for the device again.
  • If necessary, close and reopen ANGLE on both devices and try again.

Language

ANGLE supports Korean and English.

You can change the app language from Settings > Language in ANGLE.
